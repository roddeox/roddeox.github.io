R P M

Researcher. Engineer. Language enthusiast.

Seoul, South Korea

I’m a computer vision and AI engineer at Mopticon, where I build real-time perception systems for intelligent transportation systems. I completed my MS in Computer Science and Engineering at Sogang University as a Global Korea Scholarship scholar, where I worked at the Computer Vision and Image Processing Lab. I hold a BS in Mechanical Engineering from Universidade Federal Fluminense in Brazil.

I have experience in researching multimodal deep learning, computer vision, speech processing, speech visualization, and reinforcement/imitation learning for simulation. I have also contributed to the AuQuA project, a multi-institution research collaboration on AI-driven industrial quality assurance while working at Extreme Digital Solutions.

Outside of research, I’m passionate about learning languages. I speak fluent Portuguese, English, and Korean.
