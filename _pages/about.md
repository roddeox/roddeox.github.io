I'm a computer vision and AI engineer at [Mopticon](https://www.mopticon.com), where I build real-time perception systems for intelligent transportation systems. I completed my MS in Computer Science and Engineering at [Sogang University](https://www.sogang.ac.kr) as a [Global Korea Scholarship](https://www.niied.go.kr/web/niied/niiedEng/main) scholar, where I worked at the [Computer Vision and Image Processing Lab](https://cviplab.sogang.ac.kr/cviplab/). I hold a BS in Mechanical Engineering from [Universidade Federal Fluminense](https://international.uff.br/) in Brazil.

I have experience in researching multimodal deep learning, computer vision, speech processing, speech visualization, and reinforcement/imitation learning for autonomous vehicle simulation. I have also contributed to the [AuQuA project](http://auqua.wzl.rwth-aachen.de/en/default.html), a multi-institution research collaboration on AI-driven industrial quality assurance while working at [Extreme Digital Solutions](https://www2.extremedigital.com.br/).

Outside of research, I'm passionate about learning languages. I speak fluent Portuguese, English, and Korean.
